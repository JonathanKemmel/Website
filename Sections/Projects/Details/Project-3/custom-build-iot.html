Planning the Hardware

  • Define device functionality and required capabilities
  • Identify additional components needed (sensors, relays, LEDs, etc.)
  • Determine power supply solution and network connectivity method
  • Choose firmware platform (ESPHome vs Tasmota vs custom)
  • Consider physical enclosure and placement requirements
  • Plan for OTA updates and accessibility for future modifications
  • Budget estimation and component sourcing
